# Prior-art audit for the Perrin period criterion

Both defining fields have database records. The LMFDB number-field lookup on the literal H(x)=x⁹−18x⁶+54x³+27 redirects to 9.3.6466042647.1. The record gives signature (3,3), absolute discriminant 6466042647, transitive group 9T18, abstract group 108.17, and cubic subfield 3.3.621.1. Its normalized defining polynomial is x⁹−3x⁸+3x⁷−3x⁶+3x⁵+6x⁴−18x³+15x²−6x+4. The cubic subfield record uses precisely q(x)=x³−6x−3. These are catalogue entries for the fields generated by individual roots. The action on the nine roots gives the group of the degree-108 normal closure. [LMFDB exact polynomial lookup](https://www.lmfdb.org/NumberField/?jump=x%5E9-18%2Ax%5E6%2B54%2Ax%5E3%2B27) · [Nonic field](https://www.lmfdb.org/NumberField/9.3.6466042647.1) · [Cubic field](https://www.lmfdb.org/NumberField/3.3.621.1)

Kurtz, Shanks and Williams (1986), §5, pp. 700–701, explicitly recall the original Adams–Shanks divisibility of the Perrin period W(p) by the splitting-type bounds (W(p) divides p²+p+1 for I primes). Their sieve precomputed periods for the first 4,770 primes through 46,099. The section uses those periods to sieve for pseudoprimes. Thus small numerical period evidence substantially predates this work. [Paper, §5](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1986-0829639-7)

Klaška and Skula (2010), §3, give a close method for a different characteristic polynomial, the Tribonacci cubic x³−x²−x−1. Their Lemmas 3.2–3.4 use cube roots of an irreducible cubic root, Frobenius conjugates, the norm and an auxiliary cubic polynomial. Theorems 3.5–3.6 show every eligible Tribonacci root obeys τ^((p²+p+1)/3)=1. In the Perrin case both outcomes occur. These are precedents for the method and for posing the index-three question at I primes. [Paper, §3](https://www.fq.math.ca/Papers1/48-3/Klaska_Skula.pdf)

Indexed searches combined the Perrin period, plastic constant, I primes, order divisible by 3, density, and the two defining polynomials. The sources inspected did not establish previous publication of the precise link between splitting of 3.3.621.1 and index-three divisibility of the Perrin period, or of the corresponding 1/3 conditional density. Sources can use equivalent fields, class-field characters, or binary quadratic forms. No claim of priority follows from this search.
